The "Resume on Reload" Logic
The Goal: Don't just remember the time; remember the context.

onBreak: Remembers if we were resting or working. (The Mode)

initial_seconds: Remembers the full length of the timer (e.g., 25:00). (The Total)

totalSeconds: Remembers exactly where the countdown stopped (e.g., 14:32). (The Progress)

Why? If we only save #3, the progress bar thinks the timer "started" at 14:32 and looks full (100%). By saving #2 and #3, the bar knows it's only half-done.
